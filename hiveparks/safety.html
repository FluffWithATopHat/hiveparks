Jump Rules

Following these rules keeps everyone safe and having fun

General Rules & Guidelines

🦷 Grip socks are required for all jumpers at all times ($3.99/pair in-store)
🟢 All participants must have a signed waiver on file before entering
🚨 No flipping, somersaults, or advanced maneuvers unless in designated areas
🚫 One person per trampoline at a time in open trampoline courts
🔥 No roughhousing, pushing, or wrestling on trampolines or equipment
💣 Jump in control — always know where you are landing
🛒 No food, drinks, or gum permitted in the jumping area
🗹 No jewelry, sharp objects, or hard accessories while jumping
👶 Children under 7 must be supervised by an adult at all times
🎯 Follow all posted rules and staff instructions throughout the park
🤯 Exit trampolines safely — do not jump off the edges onto foam blocks
📝 Pregnant guests and those with medical conditions should consult a doctor before jumping

Age & Weight Guidelines

Safety guidelines to ensure the best experience for all guests

💣

Toddlers (Under 3)

Special toddler soft-play area available. Toddlers jump free in toddler zones with adult present.

👶

Kids (3–12)

Standard jump passes apply. Children under 7 require adult supervision at all times in the park.

👷

Teens & Adults

All standard attraction access. Weight limit of 250 lbs for some attractions — check posted signs.

Our Safety Standards

The Hive Adventure Park is committed to providing the highest standards of safety for all guests. Our park is designed, maintained, and operated in compliance with nationally recognized safety guidelines for trampoline and adventure parks.

OPHEA Certified IATP Member Regular Safety Audits Trained Safety Staff

All staff members are trained in CPR, first aid, and park-specific safety protocols. Safety monitors are positioned throughout the park at all times during operating hours.
