OUR ATTRACTIONS

An adventure awaits around every corner at The Hive

Everything Under One Roof

40,000+ square feet packed with non-stop action

🤺

Open Trampolines

Our flagship attraction — a massive open court of wall-to-wall trampolines. Jump, flip (in designated zones), and bounce to your heart’s content. Perfect for all ages!

🏀

Dodgeball Courts

Channel your inner dodgeball champion on our trampoline dodgeball courts. Organized sessions run throughout the day. Perfect for competitive fun with friends and family.

🥷

Ninja Course

Multi-lane warrior-style ninja obstacle course featuring warped walls, balance beams, ring challenges, and more. Do you have what it takes to complete the course?

🏀

Inflatable Basketball

Show off your slam dunk skills on our inflatable basketball court. Multiple hoop heights available for all ages and skill levels. Great for competitive play or just messing around!

🏛

Giant Bounce House

Our massive bounce house is perfect for younger jumpers. A huge, safe enclosed bouncing space that the kids will absolutely love. Great for birthday parties too!

🧗

Traverse Climbing Wall

Challenge yourself on our horizontal traverse climbing wall. Test your grip strength and technique as you make your way across. Great for climbers of all experience levels!

💨

Airbag Jump Pits

Launch yourself into our giant foam airbag pits for a soft, safe landing. Perfect for practicing tricks or just for the pure thrill of the jump. A fan favorite!

🏁

Giant Slides

Speed down our exhilarating giant slides for a rush of excitement. Multiple slide types available for different thrill levels. Race your friends to the bottom!

📶

Soft Play Zone

A dedicated soft-play area designed specifically for toddlers and young children. Safe, padded environment with age-appropriate activities. Parents can relax while the little ones play!

⚽

AirTrack Sport Court

A versatile inflatable sport court for basketball, soccer, and other activities. The bouncy surface adds a whole new dimension to traditional sports. Great for all athletes!

🎲

Claw Machines

Test your skills on our play until you win claw machines! Every player is guaranteed to walk away with a prize — keep trying until you grab your treasure!

📷

Photo Booth

Capture your Hive memories in our fun photo booth! Strike a pose with props and walk away with a printed keepsake to remember your adventure.
