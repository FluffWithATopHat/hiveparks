ABOUT THE HIVE

Extreme fun for all ages in Ogden, Utah

Ogden, Utah

Welcome to The Hive

The Hive Adventure Park in Ogden will be our second location and we can’t wait to meet you! Located at 145 N Harrisville Rd in Ogden, The Hive will provide extreme fun for all ages!

Attractions include a never-ending open court of trampolines and many other attractions including dodgeball, an inflatable basketball court, a large bounce house, interactive trampoline games, airbag pits, slides, soft-play, and so much more!

Want to be a ninja for the day? The Hive will also include a multi-lane ninja course! If you like basketball or soccer, the AirTrack Sport Court will be right up your alley! Feel like climbing? The Hive’s traverse climbing wall will fit the bill!

Birthday parties will also take on a whole new meaning at The Hive with full-service hosted parties available 7 days a week. All you need to bring is the cake and we will handle the rest!

Come experience some active family fun at The Hive Adventure Park!
