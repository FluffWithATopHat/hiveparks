Private Park Access

Your Group. Your Park.

Want to have the entire Hive to yourself? Group buyouts give your organization exclusive access to all 40,000+ square feet of The Hive Adventure Park. No crowds, no wait times — just pure, uninterrupted fun for your group.

Our experienced events team will work with you to create a custom experience tailored to your group’s needs, from scheduling and catering to special decorations and activities.

We accommodate groups from 20 to 300 people and offer flexible time slots including morning, afternoon, and evening sessions on weekdays and weekends.
