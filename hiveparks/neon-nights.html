What is Neon Nights?

Neon Nights is our epic weekly Friday night event where the entire park transforms into a glowing, pulsating playground! Black lights illuminate neon colors everywhere, our sound system pumps the latest hits, and the energy is absolutely electric.

Jump under the glow of UV lights, dodge neon dodgeballs on the dodgeball courts, and conquer the ninja course in full neon gear. It’s an experience unlike anything else — the perfect Friday night adventure!

What to Expect

🌞

UV Black Lights

The entire park glows under UV black lights. Wear your brightest whites and neons for maximum effect!

🎵

Epic Music

Our DJ spins the latest bangers all night long. The energy stays high from 7PM to 10PM!

🤺

Full Park Access

All attractions open during Neon Nights! Jump, ninja, dodge, climb — all glowing in UV light.
